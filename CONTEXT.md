# Domain glossary
- Sweet: a colored movable board piece.
- Stripe: a special sweet clearing a row or column.
- Parcel: a special sweet exploding twice.
- Prism: a colorless special sweet targeting a color.
- Glaze: stationary one- or two-layer coating beneath a sweet.
- Meringue: stationary layered blocker occupying a tile.
- Pulse: one simultaneous set of clears and blocker hits.
- Cascade: a new match formed after gravity/refill.
- Stage: a move-limited authored objective board.
